About ZDSPGC Dimataling Campus

Our Mission

ZDSPGC Dimataling Campus is committed to providing accessible, quality, and relevant education that transforms students into competent and ethical professionals ready to meet the challenges of the global community.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that produces graduates who are catalysts for positive change in society through excellence in instruction, research, and community engagement.

Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world. - Nelson Mandela

Our Major Courses

ZDSPGC Dimataling Campus offers three major programs designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills needed for successful careers.

Associate in Computer Technology (ACT)

A two-year program that provides students with foundational knowledge and technical skills in computer systems, programming, and information technology.

  • Computer Programming
  • Database Management
  • Network Administration
  • Web Development
Learn More

BS Information Systems (BSIS)

A four-year degree program that focuses on the design and implementation of solutions that integrate information technology with business processes.

  • Systems Analysis & Design
  • Enterprise Architecture
  • IT Project Management
  • Business Intelligence
Learn More

BS Physical Education (BPED)

A four-year program that prepares students to become competent physical education teachers, coaches, and fitness professionals.

  • Sports & Exercise Science
  • Curriculum Development
  • Health Education
  • Coaching Methodology
Learn More
